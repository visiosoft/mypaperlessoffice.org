Delete your VANLY account

This page explains how to delete your account in the VANLY app (by [Company legal name]) and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to [support@yourdomain.ae] with the subject "Delete my VANLY account", or send a WhatsApp message to [+971 XX XXX XXXX].
  2. Include the mobile number you use to sign in to VANLY. If you write from a different email or number, we will send a one-time code to your registered number to confirm it is you.
  3. We confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days. We will message you when it is done.
Email a deletion request

If you have an active rental, unpaid rent or a deposit still to be settled, we will finish that first and then delete your account. You can also ask us to delete only some data, such as your uploaded documents, without closing your account.

What happens to your data

Deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • Name, email, emirate and business name
  • Emirates ID, passport, driving licence and trade licence files
  • Fuel receipts and issue photos you uploaded
  • Message history

Kept for a limited time

  • Booking, contract, invoice and payment records: 5 years, as UAE tax and commercial law requires
  • Records linked to an open traffic fine, damage claim or dispute: until it is resolved

Kept records are stored securely, used only for these legal purposes, and deleted when the retention period ends. Card details are never stored by VANLY; they stay with Stripe, our payment provider.
