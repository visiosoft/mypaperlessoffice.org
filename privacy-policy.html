What we do not collect

  • Your device location. You choose your city yourself.
  • Your contacts, photos you don't choose to upload, or other files on your phone.
  • Advertising IDs. The app shows no ads and has no third-party analytics or tracking.

How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To process bookings, contracts, payments, deposits and refunds.
  • To verify identity and driving eligibility before handing over a van.
  • To send booking, payment and service messages by WhatsApp or SMS.
  • To provide support and handle issues, damage, fines or disputes.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations in the UAE.

We use your data only for these purposes. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe: to process card payments (stripe.com/privacy).
  • Twilio: to deliver sign-in codes and WhatsApp or SMS messages (twilio.com/legal/privacy).
  • Our hosting provider: to store data securely on our servers.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, for example the police, RTA or courts in relation to traffic fines, accidents or legal claims.

These providers may only use your data to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

  • Account and profile: while your account is open.
  • Identity and licence documents: while your account is open, and for up to 12 months after your last rental ends, in case of fines, damage or disputes.
  • Bookings, contracts, invoices and payment records: at least 5 years, as required by UAE tax and commercial record-keeping law.
  • Message logs: up to 12 months.

When data is no longer needed it is deleted or anonymised.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure storage. Documents are kept on access-controlled servers and are only visible to you and authorised VANLY staff.

Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data;
  • correct data that is wrong (you can also edit your profile in the app);
  • delete your account and data: in the app under Profile → Delete account, or see Delete your account;
  • stop or restrict certain processing.

Email [support@yourdomain.ae] from the address on your account, or message us from your registered mobile number. We reply within 30 days.

Children

VANLY is for adults and businesses. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app or by message.
